How to play
  • Your pixel's color is the one way it can move: up, down, left, right. Tap a direction to go, STOP to stop.
  • Run into a piece the same size as you and you both respawn bigger, sharing both sets of directions: 1 + 1 → 2-block, 2 + 2 → square. Different sizes just pass over each other.
  • Have a direction twice (×2)? You move that way twice as fast.
  • Go off the left or right edge and you come back on the other side one row lower. Off the top or bottom: other side, one column right.
  • Squares score by parking exactly on the hole. Then you respawn as a single pixel and a new hole appears. First to the target wins.
